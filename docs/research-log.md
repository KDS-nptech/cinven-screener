# Research log

Every data decision with its reason and date. Conclusion first on each entry.

## 9 Nov 2026

- **Register choice: Companies House, UK-headquartered universe.** Cinven is London-headquartered and the two earlier screeners share the Companies House adapter; the fund's deals are pan-European, so the limitation is stated and the extension path (one adapter per register) is in the methodology.
- **Candidates are FULL, GROUP and MEDIUM filers only.** The account category is the type of accounts last filed; small, micro, dormant and abridged categories cannot be at buyout scale. FULL includes many small audited subsidiaries, so size is verified from the accounts themselves.
- **iXBRL bulk data covers a quarter of candidates.** October 2026 archive: 1,748 matches of ~7,000 expected; September 7,735 (December year-ends). Twelve months gave 29,436 companies. The rest file PDFs; Companies House rasterises them (every PDF came back as image-only), so OCR or a vision read is required. Tesseract scan of the whole document was too slow (15 to 20 seconds a document on two cores); a contents-page read plus a vision read of the P&L page runs at 3 to 9 seconds, network-bound, four workers in parallel.
- **Document API calls use a fresh connection per document** after two workers hung for six minutes on a stalled keep-alive read through the egress proxy.
- **Employee counts scaled by filer error.** Nineteen filings carried scale="3" on the head count (9,348,000 staff at Samworth Brothers). Rule: a head count above 50,000 divisible by 1,000 is divided by 1,000 and flagged.
- **Ownership routes replace the founder-ownership gate.** The Brighton Park screener excluded investor-held companies; a buyout fund buys from them. Routes: founder or family, sponsor (with hold years from the PSC notification date), offshore vehicle, foreign parent, UK group, widely held PLC, employee-owned, public sector, uncertain.
- **Walk-up deepened from three to seven levels** after Miller (Willis chain) and Smart Communications (Platinum Newco I to III, Acquisition, Midco, Topco) stopped at the limit.
- **Sovereign wealth and pension funds read as sponsors.** Miller's PSC "Minister for Finance of the Republic of Singapore" (GIC) matched the public-body regex; sponsor detection now precedes the public-body rule and a sovereign list was added.
- **Individuals at 25 to 50% with nobody else on the register read as founder-led (low confidence).** True Potential's founder held 25 to 50% before Cinven with FTV's minority below the reporting line; the strict 50% rule called it uncertain.
- **Group heads.** 18,177 universe companies collapse onto 48,147 heads: the highest UK company in the walked chain that files GROUP accounts.
- **Gemini blocked, Anthropic used.** generativelanguage.googleapis.com is not on this session's network allowlist (device-linked session); Mistral's free tier returned 429; the user's Anthropic key works with the workspace header. Haiku 4.5 for classification, Haiku 5.5 and 4.5 pooled for the rubric, Haiku 4.5 for the vision reads.
- **Search engines.** Bing returns decoys and DuckDuckGo a CAPTCHA from this egress; Seznam returns real results at ~1 query per 2 seconds; three discovery shards.
- **Bands from the measured profile.** Flagship core (35 entries, outliers removed) EBITDA median EUR 76M, p25 61M; UK flagship operating deals EBITDA GBP 21M to 74M; Strategic Fund 2 EV ~EUR 100M to 500M. Bands: Strategic Fund GBP 8M to 30M EBITDA, flagship GBP 30M to 300M, mega-cap above. Route points favour founder or family (Fund 8 "eight out of ten primary or founder-led"; SF2 founder-owned) with sponsor-owned close behind (74% of core entries were secondaries).
- **Outliers excluded from the calibration, as the brief asked:** US-headquartered or New York-led deals (Bioclinica, Jaggaer, NSM, Drake, BioAgilytix, Nitel, Envu, Archer, Regenity, Salsify), club mega-deals (STADA EUR 5.6B, TK Elevator EUR 17.2B, MasMovil EUR 5.3B, Arxada CHF 4.2B), minority positions (Fressnapf, RTB House, Visma 2017), fund-to-fund continuations (Ufinet I and II, Viridium 2018, group.ONE 2022), and the Strategic Funds entries (measured separately: Miller, Compre, Eurovita, IFGL, Säkra, Finaxy, Policy Expert, Objectway, Flint Global, Ongoing Warehouse, Optio).
